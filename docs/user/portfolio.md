# Portfolio

The Portfolio page provides a comprehensive view of your holdings, positions, and activity on Phasor DEX.

## Overview Tab

- **Portfolio Value** — Total USD value with historical chart (1D, 1W, 1M, ALL)
- **Quick Stats** — Total value, LP positions count, 24h change

## Tokens Tab

View all tokens in your wallet with balance, price, value, 24h change, and allocation percentage.

## Positions Tab

Track your LP positions showing pool, your liquidity value, pool share, fees earned, and APR.

## Activity Tab

Transaction history including swaps, adds, removes, stakes, and claims with timestamps and explorer links.
