# vePHASOR & Voting

## What is vePHASOR?

vePHASOR (vote-escrowed PHASOR) represents your locked PHASOR tokens and the voting power they carry. When you lock PHASOR, you receive a **veNFT** — an ERC-721 NFT that holds your locked balance and tracks your voting power.

Your voting power depends on two things:
- **How much** PHASOR you lock
- **How long** you lock it for

Longer locks give you more voting power per token locked. Voting power decays linearly as your lock approaches its expiry date.

## Why Lock PHASOR?

Locking PHASOR gives you four distinct benefits:

### 1. Direct Emissions
Vote on which pools receive PHASOR rewards each epoch. Pools you vote for attract more liquidity, which increases their trading volume and fee generation.

### 2. Earn Trading Fees
You earn a share of the trading fees generated by the pools you voted for. Higher volume pools generate more fees, so voting strategically matters.

### 3. Rebase Protection
Receive additional PHASOR each epoch to offset dilution from emissions. The more total PHASOR that is locked, the higher the rebase. [Learn more →](tokenomics.md)

### 4. Launchpad Access
Only vePHASOR holders can participate in token launches on the Phasor Launchpad. Your veNFT is your ticket to early access. [Learn more →](launchpad.md)

## Lock Durations & Voting Power

Voting power scales linearly with lock duration. The maximum lock is **4 years**.

| Lock Duration | Voting Power per 100 PHASOR |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| 1 week | ~0.05 vePHASOR |
| 1 month | ~2.1 vePHASOR |
| 6 months | ~12.5 vePHASOR |
| 1 year | ~25 vePHASOR |
| 2 years | ~50 vePHASOR |
| 4 years (max) | 100 vePHASOR |

> **Formula:** Voting power = PHASOR amount × (lock duration / 4 years)

For standard locks, your voting power **decays linearly** as your unlock date approaches. At expiry, your voting power reaches zero and you can withdraw.

### Permanent Locks

You can also choose a **permanent lock**, which maintains your maximum voting power indefinitely. Your PHASOR stays locked until you explicitly disable the permanent lock option (after which normal linear decay begins).

Permanent locks are ideal if you plan to be an active, long-term participant.

## How to Lock PHASOR

1. Navigate to the **Governance** page
2. Click **Lock PHASOR**
3. Enter the amount of PHASOR to lock
4. Select your lock duration (1 week to 4 years) or choose **Permanent**
5. Review your projected voting power
6. Click **Approve PHASOR** (first time only)
7. Click **Create Lock** and confirm the transaction

You'll receive a veNFT in your wallet representing your lock.

## Voting on Gauges

Each epoch (1 week), you can allocate your voting power across one or more pools. Pools with more votes receive a larger share of PHASOR emissions.

### How to Vote

1. Go to the **Governance** page
2. View the list of available gauges (pools)
3. Allocate voting weight to the pools you want to support
4. Click **Vote** and confirm

### What Happens After You Vote

- Pools you voted for receive proportionally more PHASOR emissions next epoch
- You earn trading fees from the pools you voted for
- Votes reset each epoch — you need to vote again or your previous vote is maintained until changed

### Voting Strategy

- **Vote for high-volume pools** — They generate more trading fees, which means higher earnings for voters
- **Consider incentives** — Some pools may offer additional incentives (bribes) to attract votes
- **Diversify** — Spread votes across multiple pools to capture fees from different sources

## Managing Your veNFTs

### View Your Locks
On the Governance page, the **My Locks** section shows all your veNFTs with:
- Lock amount
- Unlock date (or "Permanent")
- Current voting power
- Active votes

### Increase Lock Amount
Add more PHASOR to an existing lock to boost your voting power without changing the unlock date.

### Extend Lock Duration
Push your unlock date further out to regain decayed voting power.

### Withdraw
After your lock expires, you can withdraw your PHASOR tokens. Permanent locks must first be converted to standard locks before the countdown begins.

## Tips

- **Lock for the maximum duration** to maximize your voting power and fee earnings
- **Vote every epoch** to ensure you're earning trading fees
- **Permanent locks** are the most capital-efficient choice for long-term participants
- **Watch for high-volume pools** — they generate the most fees for voters
