# Introduction

## What is Phasor DEX?

Phasor DEX is a decentralized exchange (DEX) built on the Monad blockchain. It enables trustless token swaps, liquidity provision, and a comprehensive DeFi ecosystem—all without intermediaries.

At its core, Phasor uses an **Automated Market Maker (AMM)** model, allowing users to trade tokens directly from liquidity pools rather than relying on traditional order books. This means you can swap tokens instantly at any time, with prices determined algorithmically based on pool reserves.

## Why Monad?

Monad is a high-performance EVM-compatible blockchain designed for speed and scalability. Phasor DEX leverages Monad's capabilities to offer:

- **Fast transactions** — Near-instant confirmations
- **Low fees** — Minimal gas costs for swaps and liquidity operations
- **EVM compatibility** — Familiar tooling and wallet support (MetaMask, etc.)

## How It Works

Phasor DEX is built on the proven **Uniswap V2** AMM design:

1. **Liquidity Providers (LPs)** deposit equal values of two tokens into a pool
2. **Traders** swap tokens through these pools, paying a 0.3% fee
3. **Fees** are distributed proportionally to LPs based on their pool share
4. **Prices** adjust automatically based on the constant product formula (x × y = k)

This model ensures:
- Continuous liquidity for any trade size
- Permissionless listing of new token pairs
- Transparent, on-chain price discovery
