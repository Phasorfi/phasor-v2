# Introduction

## What is Phasor DEX?

Phasor DEX is a next-generation decentralized exchange built on the Monad blockchain. Based on the battle-tested Velodrome V2 protocol, Phasor combines an efficient AMM with **ve(3,3) tokenomics** — a model that aligns the interests of traders, liquidity providers, and token holders into a single, self-reinforcing system.

Rather than treating liquidity and governance as separate concerns, Phasor ties them together: users who lock PHASOR tokens gain voting power over which pools receive emissions, and in return they earn the trading fees generated by the pools they support.

## Why Monad?

Monad is a high-performance EVM-compatible blockchain designed for speed and scalability. Phasor DEX leverages Monad's capabilities to offer:

- **Fast transactions** — Near-instant confirmations
- **Low fees** — Minimal gas costs for swaps and liquidity operations
- **EVM compatibility** — Familiar tooling and wallet support (MetaMask, etc.)

## Key Features

### Token Swaps
Exchange any supported token for another with transparent pricing and minimal slippage. The router automatically finds the best path through both volatile and stable pools. [Learn more →](swap.md)

### Dual AMM: Stable & Volatile Pools
Two pool types serve different needs. **Volatile pools** (0.30% fee) handle standard token pairs, while **stable pools** (0.05% fee) are optimized for pegged assets like USDC/USDT with dramatically lower slippage. [Learn more →](pools.md)

### ve(3,3) Tokenomics
Lock PHASOR tokens to receive a veNFT, vote on which pools receive emissions, and earn trading fees from the pools you support. The more you participate, the more you earn. [Learn more →](tokenomics.md)

### Staking with Time Multiplier
Stake LP tokens in gauges to earn PHASOR rewards. Phasor introduces a unique **time-based multiplier** — your rewards grow from 1x to 3x over 90 days of continuous staking. [Learn more →](staking.md)

### Ve-Gated Launchpad
Discover and participate in fair token launches. Only vePHASOR holders can contribute, ensuring participants have skin in the game and reducing bot activity. Successful launches automatically create liquidity pools and gauges. [Learn more →](launchpad.md)

### Portfolio Tracking
Monitor your holdings, LP positions, and transaction history all in one place. View historical charts and performance metrics. [Learn more →](portfolio.md)

## How It Works

Phasor's ve(3,3) model creates a virtuous cycle that rewards long-term participants:

1. **Liquidity Providers** deposit tokens into **volatile or stable** pools and receive LP tokens
2. **Traders** swap tokens through these pools, paying trading fees (0.30% or 0.05%)
3. **PHASOR lockers** receive veNFTs and vote on which pools get PHASOR emissions
4. **Trading fees** are distributed to vePHASOR holders who voted for those pools
5. **PHASOR emissions** flow to gauges based on voting results, rewarding LPs who stake

This flywheel means:
- LPs are incentivized to stake in high-volume pools
- Voters are incentivized to direct emissions toward productive pools
- The protocol grows as all participants benefit from increased activity
