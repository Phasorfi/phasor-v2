# Airdrop & Incentives Program

### Overview

Phasor is allocating 5% of the initial token supply to reward early participants who help build, test, and grow the protocol. Points are earned through on-chain activity on testnet and early mainnet, as well as off-chain contributions to the community.

The more you participate — and the earlier you start — the larger your share of the airdrop.

### How It Works

Phasor uses a points-based system. Every qualifying action earns points. At TGE, the airdrop allocation is distributed proportionally based on each participant's share of total points earned.

**Your airdrop share = Your points / Total points earned by all participants × Airdrop pool**

There are no tiers, no complicated multipliers. Simply use the protocol and contribute to the community. The earlier and more consistently you participate, the more points you accumulate.

### Earning Points

#### On-Chain Activity

Any meaningful on-chain interaction with Phasor earns points. Core protocol usage — swapping and providing liquidity — carries the most weight. Other interactions also contribute to your total.

**Highest impact:**

* **Swapping** tokens on Phasor DEX
* **Providing liquidity** to pools

**Also earns points:**

* Staking LP tokens in gauges
* Locking PHSR and creating vePHSR positions
* Voting on gauge emissions
* Participating in launchpad sales
* Claiming rewards and interacting with protocol contracts

All on-chain actions across both testnet and early mainnet are tracked automatically via your wallet address. No signup or registration is required to start earning — just connect your wallet and use the protocol.

#### Off-Chain Activity

Community contributions are also recognized:

* **Discord** — Active participation and meaningful contributions to the community (helping other users, reporting bugs, providing feedback, constructive discussions)
* **Twitter / X** — Engaging with Phasor content, sharing your experience, creating original content about the protocol
* **Feedback & Bug Reports** — Submitting quality feedback that helps improve the protocol

### Timeline

### Distribution

* **Allocation:** 5% of initial PHSR supply
* **Vesting:** TBA
* **Method:** Details on the claim process will be announced closer to TGE

### Sybil Policy

Phasor will actively scan for Sybil activity (multiple wallets controlled by the same individual to farm points). Wallets identified as Sybil will be disqualified from the airdrop. Phasor reserves the right to adjust point allocations and disqualify participants engaging in any form of manipulation.

Use the protocol genuinely. That's all you need to do.

### FAQ

**Do I need to sign up or register?** No. On-chain activity is tracked automatically by wallet address. Just connect and use the protocol.

**Is there a minimum amount of points to qualify?** No. Any level of participation earns points.

**Does testnet activity count the same as mainnet activity?** Both testnet and early mainnet activity earn points. Exact weighting will be finalized before the snapshot.

**When is the snapshot?** The snapshot date will only be announced after it has been taken. Until then, all activity counts.

**Can I lose my points?** Only if your wallet is flagged for Sybil activity or manipulation. Normal usage is never penalized.



_The airdrop program is subject to change. Final terms, snapshot date, and distribution mechanics will be confirmed before TGE. Follow our official channels for updates._
